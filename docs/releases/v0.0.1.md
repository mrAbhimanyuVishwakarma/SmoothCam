# SmoothCam v0.0.1, first public release

**Make a 30 fps webcam look like 60, and a 1080p one look like 4K.** Free, open source, and fully
on your PC.

SmoothCam adds a camera called **SmoothCam** to Windows 11. Pick it in Teams, Zoom, Meet, Discord or
OBS, and they get your webcam with the in-between frames filled in, optionally upscaled, cleaned up
and framed. No account, no telemetry, no network code.

🌐 Website: https://smoothcam.vercel.app

## Highlights

- **Up to 60 fps from a 24/30 fps webcam.** Real motion-estimated frame generation: NVIDIA Optical
  Flow hardware on GTX 16 / RTX cards, its own GPU compute path on AMD, Intel and everything else.
- **Up to 4K output** with five open-source upscalers: Auto, AMD FSR 1, NVIDIA Image Scaling,
  AMD CAS and Lanczos, plus Off. All run on any GPU and add no frames of delay.
- **One switch to turn it all off.** Apps instantly get your plain camera at its own resolution and
  frame rate. You're asked first if a call is using it.
- **Compare view.** Your camera exactly as it is on the left, SmoothCam on the right, labelled with
  each side's resolution and frame rate.
- **Effects:** noise reduction, low-light boost, background blur that follows your hair and
  shoulders without a halo, and auto-framing that pans and zooms like a camera operator.
- **Friendly with your calls:** the window steps aside automatically while another app uses the
  camera, and your settings apply instantly.

## Tested on

Windows 11 26H2, RTX 4060 Laptop + Intel UHD, built-in 1080p30 webcam. Measured through the
installed camera, the way apps open it:

- 1080p and 4K at 60 fps from a ~28 fps camera, with every upscaler
- all effects plus compare view at 2K and 4K: 60 fps
- low latency mode: 60 fps, about 23 ms added delay

Teams, Zoom and OBS haven't been tried by hand yet. If you try them, please tell us how it went.

## Known limitations

- **A phone connected with Phone Link (or any other virtual camera) usually can't be the input.**
  Windows doesn't let one virtual camera read another inside its camera service. SmoothCam marks
  such cameras "(virtual - may not work)". USB webcams, and phone apps that appear as a USB webcam,
  work.
- 4K in RGB32, which few apps ask for, reaches about 35–55 fps instead of 60. NV12, the usual
  format, runs at 60.
- Frame-rate and resolution changes reach an app the next time it opens the camera.

## Privacy

Video never leaves your PC. Face detection only finds *where* a face is and recognises no one.
The Website button just opens your browser. Full policy: `PRIVACY.txt` next to the app.
